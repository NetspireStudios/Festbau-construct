Challenges

Fast-Track Schedule

The franchise owner required an aggressive 10-month completion target to align with seasonal business planning and franchise opening commitments.

Brand Compliance Requirements

Dairy Queen's corporate standards demanded exacting compliance on materials, finishes, signage, and kitchen equipment — with no deviations permitted.

Site Constraints

Limited staging area, adjacent active commercial properties, and municipal servicing requirements created logistical complexity from day one.

Solutions

Long-Lead Procurement

Identified and ordered specialty kitchen equipment, HVAC units, and brand-specific signage packages within the first 30 days — preventing downstream delays.

Implemented milestone inspections aligned with Dairy Queen corporate standards at foundation, shell, rough-in, and finishes stages — catching deviations before they compounded.
