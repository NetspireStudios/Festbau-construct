Problem Solving

Challenges & Solutions

warning

Challenges

error
Adaptive Reuse Complexity

Converting a gymnasium into residential units involves fundamental changes to structural layout, floor levels, window openings, and unit demising walls — requiring creative engineering solutions.

error
Life-Safety Upgrades

The change of use from institutional to residential triggers extensive fire protection, egress, and accessibility upgrades to meet current Ontario Building Code requirements.

error
MEP Coordination

Routing new mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems through an existing structure with non-standard ceiling heights and limited chase space presents significant routing challenges.

lightbulb

Solutions

check_circle
Phased Execution Plan

Developed a detailed phased execution plan with clear milestone gates — allowing each phase to be fully inspected and approved before the next begins, reducing rework risk.

check_circle
Active Constraint Management

Maintaining a live constraint log with weekly reviews — identifying and resolving blockers before they impact the critical path or trigger costly rework.

check_circle
Multi-Trade Coordination

Weekly coordination meetings with all trades to resolve spatial conflicts, align installation sequences, and ensure life-safety systems are properly integrated with residential layouts.
