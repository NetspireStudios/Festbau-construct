Our Story

Built on Discipline, Driven by Trust

Founded in 2016, Festbau Construction started with a clear mission: deliver construction projects with institutional-grade controls and transparent reporting.

From affordable housing renovations to ground-up commercial builds, we've grown into a trusted delivery partner for public-sector agencies, REITs, and private investors across Ontario.

Our approach is straightforward: align scope, schedule, and budget early, communicate clearly throughout, and deliver with accountability. Every project receives the same institutional-grade discipline, regardless of size.
