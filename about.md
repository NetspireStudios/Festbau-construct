# D) How We Work

## Our Approach
Festbau applies a structured delivery model designed to reduce uncertainty and support predictable execution.

**[GRAPHIC: Process graphic with 5 steps.]**

## Delivery Process
1. **Align and define:** Confirm objectives, constraints, and success criteria.  
2. **Preconstruction controls:** Validate scope, pricing, constructability, and phasing.  
3. **Procurement and scheduling:** Establish procurement strategy, long-lead planning, and baseline milestones.  
4. **Execution management:** Lead site operations, coordination, safety, and QA and QC.  
5. **Closeout and turnover:** Drive deficiencies, documentation, commissioning support, and warranty readiness.
