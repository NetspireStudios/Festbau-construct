Delay Prevention

How PM Reduces Delays

Delays aren't inevitable — they're predictable. Our project management methodology identifies and eliminates bottlenecks before they impact your schedule.

01
Proactive Scheduling

Three-week look-ahead schedules identify constraints before they become critical-path blockers. We plan activities 21 days out and adjust daily.

02
Constraint Removal

Systematic tracking of prerequisite activities — permits, material deliveries, inspections — ensures no trade arrives on-site only to find they can't work.

03
Daily Coordination

Morning huddles and end-of-day debriefs with superintendents and trade foremen keep real-time alignment across all active work fronts.

04
Issue Escalation Protocols

Tiered escalation framework with defined response times — field issues resolved in hours, not weeks. Decision-makers are engaged at the right time.
