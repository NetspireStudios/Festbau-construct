Essential Series

Entry Flat Melamine Series

Our most economical cabinet door option—perfect for entry-level projects without sacrificing a clean, modern look. Built with a sturdy particleboard core and a durable melamine surface, this flat-panel design offers a sleek, minimalist appearance with excellent resistance to scratches and everyday wear. Ideal for rental units, budget-conscious builds, or simple contemporary spaces.

Door Style: Flat Pure White (MPW)
Door Style: Flat Natural Wood (MNW)

Essential Plus Series

Shaker Pure White

A timeless budget-friendly shaker door that combines style, durability, and value. Made with a sturdy HDF core wrapped in a smooth wrapping vinyl finish, delivers a crisp pure white look that brightens any space. The classic 5-piece shaker profile offers versatility for both traditional and modern designs, while the wrapping vinyl surface ensures easy cleaning and long-lasting resistance to moisture and wear.

Door Style: Shaker Pure White (SPW)
Core: HDF Finish: Vinyl Wrapping & Melamine
