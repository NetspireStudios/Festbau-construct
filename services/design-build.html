Is Design-Build Right for You?

Design-build consolidates design and construction under a single contract. It works best when you need speed, cost certainty, and a single point of accountability from day one.

Tight Timeline

Your project has a hard occupancy date or funding deadline. Design-build overlaps design and construction phases to compress the overall schedule.

You want one team responsible for design and construction—eliminating finger-pointing between separate architects and contractors.

Common Questions

Design-Build FAQ

In traditional design-bid-build, the owner hires an architect separately from the contractor. Design is completed first, then put out to bid, and then a contractor is selected to build it. In design-build, one entity (Festbau) holds both the design and construction contracts. This means decisions happen faster, coordination is seamless, and the owner has a single point of accountability for budget, schedule, and quality.

Design-build contracts typically use a Guaranteed Maximum Price (GMP) structure. As design progresses through our 30/60/90 checkpoints, the budget is refined and validated at each stage. By the 90% milestone, the GMP is locked in, meaning the owner knows the maximum project cost before construction begins. Any savings below the GMP can be shared with the owner depending on the contract terms.

Design-build is ideal for projects where speed, cost certainty, and coordination are priorities. This includes mid-rise residential, mixed-use commercial, institutional renovations, industrial facilities, and any project with a hard deadline or complex stakeholder requirements. If your project has tight timelines, multiple funding sources, or regulatory complexity, design-build is typically the most efficient delivery method.

Changes are managed through a formal change management process. Every proposed change is evaluated for its impact on cost, schedule, and scope before being approved. Because design and construction are under one contract, the turnaround on change evaluations is significantly faster than in traditional delivery. Our 30/60/90 checkpoint system is designed to catch and resolve most design issues early, minimizing changes during construction.
